Design decisions

  • Join key: student_id (integer). SIS is the master list, so every SIS student is kept even when LMS or Accounting rows are missing.
  • Missing data: no LMS rows → courses: []; no Accounting row → accounting: null. Both are flagged in dataQuality instead of deleting or guessing values.
  • Grades: a numeric grade (e.g. 1.50) becomes a JSON number. A non-numeric value such as INC is never forced into a number; it stays as the uppercase string "INC" and gradeType is "status". This keeps averages from being corrupted by a fake 0 or NaN, and the grade still reads as "incomplete".
  • Orphans: LMS/Accounting rows whose student_id is not in the SIS (e.g. 1005) cannot be tied to a student, so they are not merged into students. They are listed under unmatched for review.
  • Student ID: parsed to integer but treated as an identifier (never summed or averaged).
